How the racers learn

This is evolutionary policy search: 50 drivers vary look-ahead distance, steering response, pace, corner braking, and line choices. Three elites survive unchanged, offspring inherit and mutate top drivers' settings, and five newcomers preserve exploration. The AI searches the legal road surface for the fastest route from start to finish, so it can ignore drawn corners when a shorter path stays inside the boundary. Finishers rank above unfinished drivers; faster finishes rank higher. Leaving the track ends a run. The dashed green line shows the champion's actual driven path. Choose any saved track for AI training or manual driving. Each track has its own AI population, results, and champion.
