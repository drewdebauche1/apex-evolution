How the racers learn

Each driver is a 10-input, 8-hidden-neuron, 2-output feed-forward neural network. It senses near, middle, and far path direction, lane position, speed, steering angle, and the next corner; its outputs directly control steering and throttle. Three elites survive unchanged, offspring inherit and mutate the strongest networks' 106 weights and biases, and five newcomers preserve exploration. Finishers rank above unfinished drivers; faster finishes rank higher. Leaving the track ends a run. The dashed green line shows the champion's actual driven path. Everything trains locally in this browser—no API or external AI service is used.
